## Section 5 — Screen failures

### 5.1 The rule

Screen failures are reimbursed at **actual procedures performed**, valued at the Screening column
rates in Table 2A, plus 28% overhead on those procedures, plus the $125.00 participant stipend as a
pass-through. Reimbursement is **capped at two (2) screen failures per randomized participant**. At
14 randomized participants the cap permits 28 reimbursable screen failures.

Reimbursement at actual is used rather than a flat fee because screen failures on this protocol fail
at two materially different points. A participant who fails the entry gate on **EASI <16** or
**vIGA-AD <3** fails during the assessment itself, before phlebotomy and before eDiary provisioning; a
participant who fails on a central laboratory result — QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus, serology, a
disqualifying chemistry — fails days later, after the site has performed and paid for the entire
screening visit.

### 9.5 Is the eDiary covered?

It is not.

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Budgeted: eDiary provisioning and training 110.00 + compliance review 315.00 = 425.00, plus 28% overhead | 544.00 |
| Actual: 6.00 h weekly compliance review + 5.00 h outreach = 11.00 h × 62.00 | (682.00) |
| **Shortfall per participant** | **(138.00)** |

The structural reason is visible in the Schedule of Activities. The protocol requires **daily** entry of
three NRS items — Worst Pruritus, AD Skin Pain, Sleep Disturbance — from consent through Week 24, and a
key secondary endpoint depends on a **weekly average** Worst Pruritus NRS that cannot be computed from a
non-compliant participant. But the SoA marks "eDiary compliance review" at only nine visits. Between
Week 4 and Week 8, between Week 8 and Week 12, and between Week 16 and Week 20 there are three-week
gaps with no visit, no X in the SoA, and therefore no line item — but with twenty-one days of daily
data each that somebody has to watch. Across the 24-week treatment period the budget pays for **8
in-treatment touchpoints against 24 weeks of daily diary**. Sixteen weeks of compliance monitoring are
performed and not paid for. Two of Site 1047's eleven protocol deviations were eDiary compliance
deviations.

The site's negotiating position for the next protocol should be a per-participant-per-month ePRO
compliance management fee, not a per-visit review line.
