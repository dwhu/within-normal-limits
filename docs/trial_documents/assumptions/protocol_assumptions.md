## 10. Discrepancies and ambiguities found in the registry record

These are genuine features of NCT05651711 that a reader of the corpus may notice.

| # | Observation | How this protocol handles it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Enrollment arithmetic does not close.** The design module reports 726 randomized; participant flow reports 183 placebo + 543 rocatinlimab = 726; but the adverse-events module reports a Safety Analysis Set of **180 placebo + 544 rocatinlimab = 724**. Two randomized participants are therefore never dosed, and one placebo-randomized participant moves to the rocatinlimab safety group. | §10.3 defines the Safety Analysis Set by **actual treatment received**, which reproduces exactly this behaviour: 183 − 1 (crossed to rocatinlimab) − 2 (never dosed) = 180, and 543 + 1 = 544. The protocol therefore explains the record rather than contradicting it. |
| 2 | **The record confirms stratification factors that STUDY_FACTS.md flags as ASSUMED.** The outcome analyses state the CMH test was "adjusted for the stratification factors of baseline disease severity and geographic region." | The protocol uses the canon factors (region; baseline vIGA-AD 3 vs 4), which is a legitimate operationalization of "baseline disease severity". The specific region categories remain invented. |
| 3 | **The arms module never states the dose.** It says only "Rocatinlimab Dose 1 every 4 weeks (Q4W) for 24 weeks with a loading dose at Week 2." The 300 mg dose appears only in the results section group titles and descriptions. | The protocol uses 300 mg throughout, per canon. |
| 4 | **"Q4W for 24 weeks with a loading dose at Week 2" is ambiguous about the number of doses.** Read literally it could imply a dose at Week 24. | Canon resolves this to **seven doses with no dose at Week 24**. The protocol states this explicitly in the synopsis, the schema, §6.2, and Appendix G, because it is the single most likely operational misreading. |
| 5 | **The registry's stated study end (27-AUG-2024) and the 24-week design imply a follow-up period that the record never describes.** The record gives no visit schedule and no follow-up duration; the AE time frame says "median time on trial 24.1 weeks (min 0.6, max 40.3)". | The 40.3-week maximum is consistent with the canon ~40-week total participation (≤30 d screening + 24 w treatment + 12 w follow-up), which the protocol reproduces. This is a corroboration, not a discrepancy. |
| 6 | **The record lists 29 secondary outcome measures but gives no indication which were alpha-controlled.** Several are near-duplicates differing only in timepoint or threshold. | §10.5 invents a Gate 2 ordering and states explicitly that endpoints outside the controlled family carry nominal p-values. Flagged Low confidence. |
| 7 | **"151 centers" (results) vs "197 locations" (contacts/locations module).** | STUDY_FACTS §3 already reconciles this (151 activated centers, 197 listed locations). The protocol uses only the prospective "approximately 150 centers". |
| 8 | **The record's eligibility criteria are unusually sparse for a Phase 3 study** — six inclusion and three exclusion criteria, all of which concern disease severity and washout.
