**Note on Form FDA 3454.** Forms 3454 and 3455 are applicant-level submissions filed with a marketing
application, both signed by the applicant's authorised representative rather than by the investigator.
**Form 3454 is a *certification*** — an assertion that no listed investigator had a disclosable
arrangement. **Form 3455 is a *disclosure*** — an itemisation of the arrangements that do exist, with
the steps taken to minimise potential bias. A wholly negative result across all investigators is
normally certified on 3454; 3455 is used where at least one interest must be disclosed. Amgen collects
investigator-level information in 3455 format at each site, as above, so that it can compile whichever
applicant-level form the aggregate result requires. A disclosable interest is **not** disqualifying —
it is information FDA weighs when evaluating the reliability of the data.

**One-year tail.** The obligation to update runs to approximately **27-AUG-2025**, one year after last
participant last visit (27-AUG-2024). This obligation survives site close-out.

## 6. WHAT TO CHECK BEFORE FILING

The five errors that most often appear on this form:

1. **A person on the delegation log who should be in Block 6, and isn't.** Read the log backwards:
   does anyone not named in Block 6 determine eligibility, assess an adverse event, judge causality,
   prescribe, or perform the EASI/vIGA-AD rating? If so, the form is wrong and every assessment that
   person performed is exposed.
2. **A person in Block 6 who shouldn't be there.** Coordinators, the regulatory coordinator, the
   pharmacist, and the phlebotomist do not belong here. Every unnecessary name creates a permanent CV,
   licence, training, and financial disclosure obligation for no regulatory benefit.
3. **A blank Block 8.** It looks like an instruction, so it gets skipped. One of the two phase boxes
   must be marked; for this study it is the Phase 2 or 3 box.
4. **A missing facility or laboratory.** The in-office CLIA-waived urine pregnancy testing makes this
   site a laboratory facility under Block 4. Any location where IP is stored or a study procedure is
   performed belongs in Block 3.
5. **A revision date falling after the activity it was meant to authorise.** Check that Revision 1
   (14-MAR-2023) precedes Dr Nakamura's first study assessment, that the original (05-DEC-2022)
   precedes the first screening visit (11-JAN-2023), and that the signature is the investigator's own —
   not a coordinator's, not a delegate's, not "per".
